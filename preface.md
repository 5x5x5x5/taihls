# Preface — why this book

<!-- PLACEHOLDER — your motivation for writing the book. The voice below sets the house style: warm, first-person-plural, honest. -->

Artificial intelligence is changing how we diagnose disease, design medicines, and
understand life itself. Yet most introductions are written either for people who
already know calculus and programming, or for nobody in particular — full of hype
and short on understanding.

We wrote this book for the student who is ready to *understand*, not just to be
impressed. We believe you can grasp the real ideas behind AI in biology and
medicine with high-school algebra and curiosity, provided someone explains each
idea through a concrete example before reaching for the formula. That's the promise
we make on every page.
