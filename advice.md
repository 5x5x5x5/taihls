# Advice to the student

<!-- PLACEHOLDER — keep or rewrite. These habits are the ones the book is built around. -->

```{tip}
The single most important habit: **run the code, then break it.** Change a number,
re-run, and predict what will happen before you look. You learn far more from a
surprising result than from a correct one you expected.
```

A few more suggestions:

- **Read the example before the formula.** Every concept starts with a real
  health or life-science situation. Make sure that story makes sense to you first.
- **Do the exercises.** They're short and they're where the learning actually
  happens. Try before you open the solution.
- **It's fine to be confused.** Confusion is the feeling of learning something
  real. Sit with it for a few minutes before seeking help.
- **You don't need to memorize.** You need to understand well enough to look
  things up and use them.
