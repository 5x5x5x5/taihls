# Acknowledgements

**TAIHLS** builds on the generosity of the open-science, open-source, and computing-education communities:

- **Publishing & Interactive Infrastructure:** Built with [Jupyter Book 2](https://jupyterbook.org/) and the [MyST Markdown](https://mystmd.org/) engine, with browser execution powered by [JupyterLite](https://jupyterlite.readthedocs.io/) / Pyodide and GPU labs hosted via Google Colab.
- **Scientific Python Ecosystem:** NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, scikit-learn, SciPy, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, and the MedMNIST benchmark team for making real biomedical computation accessible to every student with a browser.
- **Pedagogical Inspiration:** Informed by Sue Sentance's **PRIMM** computing-education framework, Gerd Gigerenzer's work on natural-frequency health numeracy, UC Berkeley's *Computational and Inferential Thinking* (Data 8), and the open clinical-AI reproducibility community behind **TRIPOD+AI**.
- **Color Accessibility:** All figures use Masataka Okabe and Kei Ito's colorblind-safe palette so visual comparisons remain clear for every reader.
